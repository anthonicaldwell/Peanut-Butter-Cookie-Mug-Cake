# Peanut Butter Cookie Mug Cake Recipe

This is a recipe for a small peanut butter cake you can make in your microwave.

Makes for a 400ml ・ 16 ounce mug.

- 1 Whole Medium Egg

- 30ml ・2 tablespoons Peanut Butter

- 30ml ・2 tablespoons Real Raw Honey

- 60ml ・4 tabespoons

Mix until there are no chunks left.

Microwave for 90 seconds.

Enjoy.
